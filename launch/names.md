# Brand names

Five short names, each with a .com idea. **I have not checked availability.** Check each one yourself at Cloudflare Registrar, and search the name plus "bookkeeping" to make sure nobody is already using it in this space. Before you commit, also run a quick trademark search at https://tmsearch.uspto.gov.

Two rules all five follow:

- **No "tax" or "CPA" in the name.** "CPA" is a licensed title in every US state, and "tax" implies tax preparation, which this service is not.
- **Plain words a landlord can spell after hearing it once.**

| # | Name | .com idea | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Keyclose** | keyclose.com (fallback: keyclosebooks.com) | Your repo name. "Close the books" plus "keys" (rentals). Short and distinctive. |
| 2 | **Tieout** | tieoutbooks.com | The tie-out is what you actually do. Accountants will recognise the word, and landlords learn it from the page. |
| 3 | **Doorledger** | doorledger.com | Landlords count "doors". Says rentals and bookkeeping in one word. |
| 4 | **Rentclose** | rentclose.com (fallback: rentclosebooks.com) | Year-end close for rental owners. Plain and descriptive. |
| 5 | **Cleansheet Rentals** | cleansheetrentals.com | What the CPA receives: a clean sheet. Friendly, not technical. |

Once you've chosen one, replace `{{BRAND}}` in `site/index.html` (5 places). The workbook titles say "Year-End Rental Books", which is a description, not a brand, so they can stay as they are.
